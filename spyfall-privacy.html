Privacy Policy for Spyfall Playhouse

Last Updated: September 2, 2026

1. Overview

Spyfall Playhouse (the "App") is a multiplayer social deduction party game. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of data when you use our App.

2. Data Collection and Use

The App sends the display name you choose and gameplay session data (room codes, votes, round state) to our backend service so that players in the same room can play together in real time. This data is not linked to a persistent account: the App does not require sign-in, does not collect your real name, email, contact list, or any other personally identifiable information, and does not include third-party advertising SDKs.

3. Permissions

The App requests network access to connect to our multiplayer server. No other device permissions are requested.

4. Security

Gameplay and room data is transmitted over an encrypted connection to our backend. We do not sell or share this data with third parties.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
